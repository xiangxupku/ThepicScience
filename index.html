                    What this actually does

                    It reads your picture on your own device

                    The text reading happens inside your browser. Your photo is never uploaded, because there is no server behind this — it is one web page.

                    It explains from a real science library

                    Twenty-seven topics written in plain words: cells, respiration, enzymes, forces, circuits, the water cycle, DNA and more. If your topic is not in there yet, the app says so instead of making something up.

                    It never asks who you are

                    No account, no email, no price. Built by a 7th grader who thinks understanding science should be free.
